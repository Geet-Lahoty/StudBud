## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an academic, editorial environment rooted in intellectual rigor and sensory craft. Designed for focused scholars, researchers, and long-form thinkers, it eschews fleeting synthetic tropes—avoiding cold fluorescent blues, violet neon gradients, and sterile high-gloss interfaces. 

The aesthetic is anchored in traditional publishing and tactile artifact: heavy linen stock, hand-bound monographs, deep fountain-pen ink, and natural mineral pigments. It merges mid-century editorial precision with contemporary digital ergonomics. The interface should feel collected rather than assembled, patient rather than hurried, and humanely structured rather than algorithmically generated.

Interactions evoke physical quietude: micro-transitions mimic the subtle absorption of ink into heavy stock, cards behave like archival study trays, and structural grids echo classical margins found in academic presses.

## Colors

The palette operates on natural light dynamics, utilizing organic dyes and paper stocks:

- **Canvas & Backdrops**: Grounded in `#FAF8F5` (raw parchment) with secondary structural banding in `#F4EFEB` (milled linen).
- **Ink (Primary / Text)**: `#1F2421` (deep carbon spruce-ink), providing high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure black. Secondary copy uses a lowered density ink `#4A524D`.
- **Terracotta (Secondary / Energy)**: `#C85A32` (calcined brick/rust), dedicated to primary calls-to-action, key focus rings, and high-priority scholarly annotations. Supported by muted amber `#D4A373` for highlights.
- **Deep Sage & Olive (Tertiary / Balance)**: `#2D5A46` (library buckram cloth), used for verified states, archival indexing, and academic citations.
- **Surfaces & Borders**: Primary reading containers rest on `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F5F0` (warm bone). Separators and framing rules employ `#E6DFD5`—a hairline boundary that reads like a scored crease rather than a digital wireframe.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional dialogue between the narrative flow of traditional printing and the navigational clarity of modern scholarship.

- **Headlines (`Source Serif 4`)**: Authoritative, stable, and sculpted. Used for article titling, thesis headers, and chapter indexing. Uses slight optical kerning adjustments to recreate hot-metal impression weight.
- **Reading & Long-Form Body (`Newsreader`)**: Tuned for sustained, deep reading sessions. Generous vertical line metrics (`1.55`–`1.6x`) prevent cognitive fatigue and encourage active margin note-taking.
- **Metadata & Controls (`Work Sans`)**: Humanist, neutral, and unmannered. Applied strictly to structural chrome, indexes, timestamps, pagination, and data points. Set with deliberate positive letter-spacing (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) to remain sharp at microscopic sizes against linen backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an academic folio: structured yet uncrowded, with dedicated zones for primary study, lateral research notations, and persistent indexing.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and wider)**: An asymmetric 12-column grid. Main texts utilize an 7-column spine flanked by a 3-column marginalia zone for footnoting, citations, and scholarly apparatus, framed by `margin-lg` outer margins.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px – 1279px)**: An 8-column layout. Marginalia collapses into collapsible inline drawers or pull-out study trays; gutters tighten to `gutter`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: A 4-column reading canal anchored by `margin` outer guards. Full horizontal bleed for tactile cards, retaining a unified single-scroll reading column.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Vertical cadence uses baseline multiples of 4px and 8px. Spacing between paragraphs and sectional headers prioritizes top-heavy clearance (`space-xl` above headings, `space-sm` below) to visibly anchor thematic chapters to their supporting paragraphs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial neon drop-shadows and thick dark scrims. Instead, elevation is rendered through **tonal stratification, paper layering, and diffused mineral ambient shadows**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: `#FAF8F5` matte paper base.
- **Level 1 (Study Trays & Document Panes)**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F5F0` crisp cards outlined by a hairline border (`1px solid #E6DFD5`). Surfaces receive a gentle, warm downward wash: `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Contextual Citations & Floating Footnotes)**: Raised natural parchment with an amplified ambient spread: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.08)`.
- **Tactile Inset (Input Well & Scored Grooves)**: Elements sunk into the canvas use a soft debossed effect created with subtle interior tones (`#F0EAE1`) and top border weighting.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** standard. Radii are kept crisp and restrained (4px default for interactive components, 8px for foundational reading containers), mirroring guillotine-cut paper, book boards, and archival slipcases. 

Avoid high-radius pill buttons or circular bubbles, which degrade the formal, intellectual composure of the interface. Badges and chips remain clipped rectangular forms with softened corners, preserving structural alignment with surrounding text columns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep ink (`#1F2421`) background with cream text (`#FAF8F5`). Rectangular with `0.25rem` radius. Subtle active press state drops Y-axis translation by 1px with no artificial glow.
- **Secondary / Action**: Warm terracotta outline (`1.5px solid #C85A32`) with terracotta text on transparent base; fills to faint wash (`rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.06)`) on hover.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Raw spruce-ink text with an understated underline scored 2px beneath the baseline in `#E6DFD5`, darkening to `#1F2421` upon cursor entry.

### Chips & Academic Badges
- Non-pill, architectural tags (`border-radius: 2px`).
- Backgrounds use low-saturation vegetable wash: `#F4EFEB` base with `#4A524D` text for standard classifications; pale olive wash (`rgba(45, 90, 70, 0.08)`) with `#2D5A46` text for verified sources; faint ochre wash (`rgba(212, 163, 115, 0.15)`) for draft/annotation highlights.
- Bordered by a delicate `1px solid currentColor` at 20% opacity.

### Cards & Reading Vessels
- Rendered on `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F5F0` with crisp `1px solid #E6DFD5` perimeter framing.
- Header bands are partitioned with hairline dividers rather than elevated containers. Internal padding maintains a generous `space-lg` breathability.

### Input Fields
- Inputs emulate tactile index ledger entries: `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5` surface with a continuous `1px solid #D8CFC4` border.
- Focus state switches the border to `#C85A32` accompanied by a flat `2px` ring in `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.15)`—no blurring. Labels use `Work Sans` (`label-md`) anchored cleanly above the frame.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square with softened corners (checkbox) and sharp concentric circles (radio).
- Default: unselected state bordered by `#4A524D` on ivory base.
- Selected: filled with `#1F2421` displaying a crisp bone checkmark (`#FAF8F5`), ensuring immediate, unambiguous contrast.

### Lists & Citations
- Academic list items employ hanging indents or warm terracotta chapter numerals in `Source Serif 4`. 
- Separated by soft horizontal rules (`1px solid #F0EBE1`) extending to margin boundaries without aggressive termination.

### Marginalia & Annotation Drawer
- Specialized side-rail containers for commentary, footnote collation, and contextual apparatus. Features a continuous left vertical rule in `#E6DFD5` and typographic scaling pegged strictly to `body-sm` and `caption`.
